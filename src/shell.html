Click a valve, igniter or tank with the Select tool to change its settings: which number key runs it, open/closed, start delay, length, direction, gas and pressure. Press H or Esc to close.

Drag parts in from the left: they snap together. Drag a part to move it and everything under it (Alt: just that one). Shift+wheel stretches a tank or booster; Ctrl+wheel or [ ] makes a part wider or narrower. Del removes. M mirror.

Vessel

Ascent autopilot

Flies a gravity turn to orbit, throttles down around Max-Q, stages by itself, then circularises at apoapsis.

Maneuver planner

Pick a burn: it shows on the map (M) as a dashed red path. Execute warps to it, points the right way and burns.

Landing autopilot

Stops the sideways speed over the target, falls, then a suicide burn with the legs down: under 3 m/s at touchdown. Works on Earth (landing zone) and the Moon.

Time & weather

Warp to: jumps time ahead and stops right before it. Works on the ground too (wait for sunrise or better weather: each launch from the editor gets new weather).

SAS · attitude hold

Keys 1–9. Steering with A D drops back to Stability. Holds use the rocket's own turning power: fins can win in thick air.
